The Green River

Salt flats. Warning boards. Graves nobody comes back to tend.

Ral Koda had a creek in it once. Thirty years of missing rain later it is called the Glare, a white flat that runs to the horizon whichever way you turn and gives nothing back, and the south is that and a handful of dying towns strung along the last of the water.

North of all of them stands a wall of trees a hundred floors high. It runs east until the eye gives up and west until the eye gives up, and nobody has ever gone into it and come out. The track up to it is lined with boards telling you to turn around, and with the graves of the people who did not. From the outside it looks like the only living thing left on the map.

Dren goes in with a sword, a satchel, and a pack of cheap cloth ribbons to tie on branches so that he can find his way back out.

Ten steps in, he cannot see the river. Twenty steps in, he cannot see the ribbon.
